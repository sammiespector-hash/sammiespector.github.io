Sammie Spector

Sammie currently leads Content Design at Checkout.com. Previous hats include leading UX Content Design teams at Angi and Condé Nast, and a UX Design team at Vogue.

She directs design practices that shape how products speak, while building the craft, the culture, and the systems underneath.

Experience

My UX career grew hand-in-hand with the industry; I was the very first content design intern at Intuit in Silicon Valley. From there, I worked in FinTech at Intuit and Morningstar, Inc. then jumped to Publishing and Interactive Media. Now, I'm working on digital marketplaces for the broadest range of users yet, homeowners and tradespeople across the globe.

Agentic Content Design Projects

Team Leadership & Agentic Design 02Condé Nast

Global Accounts

I redesigned the end-to-end sign-in flow across 10+ Condé Nast brands and 6 markets. This resulted in a 500% increase in signed-in users and 6.3M new registered users across the globe.

Content Systems & Flexible Components 03Vogue

Vogue Open Text Search

I led content design for Vogue's AI-powered image search — a technically-minded project that proves content design matters even when there's barely any text on the screen. The feature alone drove 17% of monthly paywall conversions and 70% of all app sessions.

AI & Content Systems 04Vogue

Paywall Design

I crafted Vogue's first-ever digital paywall experience. I empathized with charging people money, so I had to balance a disruptive experience with the overarching business model. This new gated experience drove 61% of total site subscriptions.

Strategy & Conversion 05Vogue

Met Gala Voting Experience

I designed an interactive voting feature for the Met Gala red carpet. It was my first foray into game theory. Funny enough, it was also my last. It generated 2.9M votes and 27k registrations, which was 83% above our goal, without frustrating non-participating (no fun) readers.
